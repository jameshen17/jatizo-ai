隐私政策 · Privacy Policy

更新于 Updated: 2026-09-16

数据保存在设备上 · On-device data

你选择的城市、称呼、联系窗口和重要事件保存在本机，App 与 Widget 通过共享容器读取这些数据。没有用户账号、多人同步、广告或行为分析 SDK。 Your selected cities, names, contact window and important event are stored on your device and shared locally with the widget. The app has no account, multi-user sync, advertising or analytics SDK.

天气与城市搜索 · Weather and city search

App 和 Widget 向 Apple WeatherKit 发送所选城市的坐标以获取天气；App 不读取设备 GPS 或追踪你或家人的位置。城市名称与别名优先在本机搜索；使用 Apple 搜索补充时，搜索词会发送给 Apple。称呼、事件名称和联系安排不会附在这些请求中。Apple 服务按其隐私条款处理请求。 The app and widget send the selected cities’ coordinates to Apple WeatherKit to retrieve weather. The app does not read device GPS or track you or your family. City names and aliases are searched locally first; Apple search sends the search query to Apple. Personal names, event titles and contact plans are not attached to these requests. Apple processes service requests under its privacy terms.

提醒与控制 · Reminders and control

提醒由 iOS 在本机安排，需要你的通知授权。你可以编辑或删除安排、关闭提醒，或在系统设置中关闭通知。删除安排会取消其待发送提醒。App 不提供自己的云端数据存储；设备备份由你的系统设置控制。 Reminders are scheduled locally by iOS with your permission. You can edit or delete plans, disable reminders, or turn off notifications in system settings. Deleting a plan cancels its pending reminders. The app provides no developer-operated cloud storage; device backups are controlled by your system settings.

支持邮件与网页 · Support email and website

主动发送支持邮件时，我们会收到你的邮箱地址和邮件内容，仅用于处理你的请求。请不要发送不必要的家庭或敏感信息。你可以通过下方邮箱请求删除支持邮件。访问支持网页时，托管服务可能处理 IP 地址等必要的访问日志；本页面不加入广告或分析脚本。 If you email support, we receive your email address and message to handle your request. Avoid sending unnecessary family or sensitive information. You may request deletion of support correspondence at the address below. The website hosting service may process necessary access logs, including IP addresses; these pages include no advertising or analytics scripts.
