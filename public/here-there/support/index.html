两地设置 · Your two places

点击首页 Here 或 There 卡片，先填写称呼，再选择城市。支持中文和英文城市搜索。旅行后请手动更新地点；已有安排保留原来的基准城市和时区。 Tap a Here or There card to enter a name and select a city. Search in Chinese or English. Update cities manually when traveling; existing plans retain their original anchor city and time zone.

联系提醒 · Contact reminders

选择联系窗口、重复星期和基准城市后打开提醒。App 最多预排未来 60 次联系提醒；每次打开 App 自动续排，并在首页显示实际安排截止日期。长期不打开 App，排期用完后不会继续提醒。夏令时导致起点或终点不存在的窗口会跳过；重复的钟点采用第一次。 Choose a contact window, weekdays and anchor city, then enable reminders. Up to 60 future contact reminders are scheduled. Opening the app renews the queue and shows the actual coverage end date on the home screen. Reminders stop when the queue runs out if you do not reopen the app. Windows with a nonexistent start or end during daylight saving changes are skipped; repeated times use the first occurrence.

重要时刻 · Important moment

事件按 There 的当地时间录入，并同时显示两个时区。提醒在事件前 30 分钟触发；如果提醒时间已过，不会补发。事件与联系窗口均由你手动设置，不代表对方实时有空。 Enter an event in There’s local time and see it in both time zones. An optional reminder is scheduled 30 minutes before the event; a past reminder is not sent late. Events and contact windows are your own plans, not live availability from another person.

Widget 与天气 · Widget and weather

在 iPhone 主屏幕添加 Here & There 中号 Widget。轻点地点或安排可打开对应编辑页。Widget 刷新由 iOS 调度；天气和日期可能延迟更新，打开 App 可刷新。日夜配色使用当地日出日落，缺少有效数据时按当地 06:00–18:00 判断白天。 Add the medium Here & There widget to your iPhone home screen. Tap a place or plan to open its editor. iOS controls widget refresh; weather and dates may update with a delay. Open the app to refresh. Day and night colors follow local sunrise and sunset, falling back to 06:00–18:00 when valid solar data is unavailable.

需要帮助？ · Need help?

请说明问题、App 版本、iPhone 型号和 iOS 版本；截图可以帮助定位问题，请先遮盖个人信息。 Describe the issue and include the app version, iPhone model and iOS version. Screenshots can help; hide personal information first.
